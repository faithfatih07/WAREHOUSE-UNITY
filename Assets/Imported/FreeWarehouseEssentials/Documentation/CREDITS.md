This folder contains 14 selected interactive FBX models and their referenced texture/material maps, plus the 17 separate animation-sample FBXs and creator documentation. The raw animation samples are retained as reusable assets. The nested Unity package and Blender project are not included or opened. The creator's original `LICENSE.txt` is retained unchanged.

The six previously verified models remain: `Storage_Shelf`, `Crate_Large`, `Pallet`, `Pallet_Jack`, `Roller_Conveyor` and `Workbench_Vise`. Eight additional unique models were selected from the same CC0 collection: `Box_Medium`, `Box_Small`, `Crate_Small`, `Double_Locker`, `Electrical_Cabinet`, `Open_Carton`, `Toolbox` and `Wooden_Spool_Table`. Import hashes for the eight additions and all 17 animation samples matched the files in the reviewed archive. Unity import and playback results are recorded in `UnityImportVerification.md` and `AnimationPlaybackValidation.md`.
